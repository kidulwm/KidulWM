# LAW — naming (RatuKidulTech / Kidul Systems / KidulWorks) (LOCKED 2026-11-14, Barticus present, GSI)

One name, one meaning. Three layers, one house. SlideDeck and KSD never leave
the repo; Granny sees only "Kidul".

## The three layers

  RatuKidulTech  = the COMPANY / the owner. "Ratu Kidul" is the Queen of the
                   Southern Sea — the house itself, and the wife's mark. The
                   legal entity everything else sits under.
  Kidul Systems  = the PRODUCTS. Networking / security / OS. The public face —
                   what you buy.
  KidulWorks     = the CODE. B&B's workshop — you & me, coding until early
                   morning. The maker-mark, never the storefront.

Three names, three jobs, zero overlap: owner = RatuKidulTech, products =
Kidul Systems, code = KidulWorks.

## The families (SystemFam / OwnersFam / KidulCrew)

"Family" is a loaded word — it must never mean two things at once. Three
buckets, kept apart on purpose:

  SystemFam   = the GEAR. TV / router / server — "the family of systems"
                (kidulSystems' hardware: one system, many roles).
  OwnersFam   = the RESIDENTS. Dad / Mum / kid — the family you LIVE with.
                The Sign In "Residents" list.
  KidulCrew   = the non-residents. The crew you HANG with — like-minded
                people who drop by, key in, and their external drive rides
                in. The Sign In "KidulCrew" door (sign-in, no list).

"Family you live with → Crew you hang with." The Systems family and the
Owners family are never conflated: the gear is SystemFam, the household is
OwnersFam, and the wider circle is the KidulCrew.

The OS is not a new thing per device — it is ONE system, differentiated by the
INSTALL (which role gets laid down). "Kidul <Category>" is the honest name: it
says what the thing is. The category word is PERMANENT — never dropped "once it
is a household name"; it is the honest descriptor and the colour anchor. Granny
buys "a Kidul TV", not "a Kidul".

  Kidul TV        = the computer TV — Granny's box, the home device.
  Kidul PC        = the desktop / laptop.
  Kidul Router    = the mesh edge (krouter).
  Kidul Server    = the headless workhorse.
  ...             = every further role adds its own "Kidul <Category>".

## "Kidul" — the interoperability word

The single word "Kidul" is the adjective that matters in the real world: "is it
a Kidul?" = "can I plug in my hard drive and my approved peripherals, and use
your screen to do my work?" — like "is it USB-C?" or "is it a Mac?". "Does your
hotel chain have Kidul TVs, so I can leave the laptop home and bring just a
drive, a remote and a keyboard?" The word earns its meaning as a compatibility
promise, not a logo.

## The colour — the category IS a colour

Each category carries the SAME ramp as the environment (theme.md Q.E): ONE colour
ramp, ONE meaning — containment depth / character, from home to edge.

  Kidul TV        = ORANGE #d9722e         the home — protect it.
  Kidul PC        = LIME   #32cd32         the pen — general, kid-safe.
  Kidul Server    = ELECTRIC BLUE #1e90ff  the brig — serves the wild.
  Kidul Router    = ELECTRIC PURPLE #bd00ff the plank — the edge, locked tight.

The device's colour IS its character (its primary environment). An app's glow and
a device's identity draw the SAME ramp — orange = home, purple = edge. This is
why "Kidul as a colour" works: the colour is the family, the shade is the
category. One ramp, one meaning, everywhere.
