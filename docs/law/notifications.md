# LAW — notifications are a top marquee, not a peek

LOCKED 2026-11-11 (Barticus: "notifications marquee across the top ... just so
granny can see every word cleanly", "gsi").

Amends the peek rework direction (2026-11-11): the peeks were going to carry
notifications on the right side. Wrong container. This law retires that and
moves notifications to a top marquee.

## The sentence

A notification is a message Granny must be able to READ, not an icon to
notice. It renders as a marquee across the TOP of the screen — full-width,
uninterrupted, every word cleanly legible from the lounge. The narrow side
peek is the wrong shape for prose; it turns a sentence into a cramped,
truncated sliver.

## The law

1. NOTIFICATIONS = TOP MARQUEE. A notification scrolls as a marquee across
   the top edge of the screen, full width, at a size Granny can read. It is
   text-first, never an icon-first badge trapped in a peek.

2. THE PEEK DOES NOT CARRY PROSE. The side peeks are for glyphs, gauges and
   one-line status — never a multi-word notification. A notification that
   can't be read in full is a notification that failed.

3. CFC-PENDING IS THE CATCH-UP, NOT THE DEFAULT. When a notification is
   pending/undelivered, it may be CFC'd (centred, focus attention) rather
   than marquee'd — but the marquee is the everyday read path. Both forms
   must be legible from the lounge.

4. CHROME LAW STANDS. The marquee is chrome: it retreats with the rest of
   the chrome during fullscreen/attention (fullscreen.md), and it must not
   steal input from the focused slide (INPUT LAW — arrows/Enter belong to
   the app, not the marquee).

## Open after lock

- Exact marquee motion/behaviour (speed, pause-on-hover, queue-of-many) is a
  later visual cut, not this law. The locked fact is the TOP marquee surface
  and the "every word cleanly legible" bar.
- The PC-top-pill-bar question (whether notifications share it) is still
  unthought — this law covers the TV surface only; PC is a separate ruling
  when Barticus gets there.

## What stays / what dies

- STAYS: the peek rework direction itself (widgets grow, move to screensaver/
  games/server) — this law only relocates notifications OUT of the peek.
- DIES: "right peek = notifications" — the icon-in-peek idea, retired.
